Your route from Jaipur to Bandipur
A forest-edge holiday centred on a pre-arranged safari and time in the Bandipur landscape, suitable for families who prefer fewer transfers and a nature-focused stay.
Connection routing may involve a change depending on available services. Confirm the arrival details before committing to a same-day outstation transfer.
Step 1: travel from Jaipur
Request assistance with an available connection to the agreed tour starting point. Share preferred departure windows and whether you want bus, train or flight options. Route availability is checked for the dates; the page does not assume a direct service.
Step 2: meet your private vehicle
Send the terminal, station exit or bus arrival point and allow time to collect luggage. The road tour starts after the agreed pickup. If the connection ends in Bangalore for a Mysuru-starting itinerary, the onward transfer must be explicitly included in the quotation.
Step 3: explore Bandipur
Forest-road movement and safari reporting times govern arrival planning; wildlife sightings are uncertain and forest access must be reconfirmed for travel dates. The final selection is agreed around the guests’ interests, hotel position, mobility and available daylight; optional experiences are quoted and confirmed separately.
Indicative onward road travel from Bangalore: 5–6 one way. Your package duration includes both the onward and return road journeys from the agreed city.
Step 4: return and reconnect
Choose a return connection after allowing a realistic road-travel buffer. A flight, train or bus ticket should not be booked against an unconfirmed final-day sightseeing schedule.

Frequently asked questions
How does the Bandipur package from Jaipur work?
Request a connection from Jaipur to the agreed Bangalore pickup, followed by the private Bandipur itinerary. Connection tickets, any additional transfer and the local tour are confirmed separately in the complete quotation.
Are Jaipur tickets included in the displayed starting price?
No. Displayed prices relate to the private vehicle-and-sightseeing ground tour from the stated starting city, based on four Sedan passengers. Origin-city tickets and accommodation are quoted separately.
Can I choose a vehicle other than a Sedan?
Yes. Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, Hycross, 12-seater Tempo Traveller, Urbania and bus options can be requested. The suitable category depends on passengers, luggage and availability.
What does the displayed starting price cover?
Where a starting price is shown, it is a vehicle-and-sightseeing transport estimate for four passengers sharing an AC Sedan. Hotels, tickets to the starting city and other trip-specific services are quoted separately.
Can the price change for my dates?
Yes. Demand, vehicle availability, travel dates, pickup and drop timings, route and requested services can change the quotation. Confirm the written inclusions and final amount before paying.
Can you arrange accommodation?
Yes, accommodation assistance can be requested. Select Yes in the form and share the room preference, adults, children and child ages so suitable stay options can be quoted.
Can you arrange my travel to Bangalore?
Yes. Request bus, train or flight assistance from your origin city. Available connections and fares are checked for the dates; a direct service to Bangalore is not assumed.
Are children included in the passenger count?
Yes. Enter all adults and children and provide each child’s age. Passenger count, luggage and any child-seat request help determine a suitable vehicle; confirm arrangements before booking.
Is a Bangalore connection suitable for every trip from Jaipur?
Connection routing may involve a change depending on available services. Confirm the arrival details before committing to a same-day outstation transfer. Compare alternatives with the team before confirming; the selected route should suit your dates and complete itinerary.
When is my booking confirmed?
An enquiry or WhatsApp message is not a confirmed reservation. Booking is confirmed after the itinerary, available vehicle, inclusions, payment arrangements and cancellation conditions have been agreed.
