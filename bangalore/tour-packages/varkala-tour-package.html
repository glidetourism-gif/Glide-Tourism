Frequently asked questions
How many days should I choose for Varkala from Bangalore?
The planned options are 5 Days / 4 Nights. These include the journey from and back to Bangalore. Confirm arrival and departure times before choosing.
How long is the drive to Varkala from Bangalore?
Allow approximately 14–16 one way as an indicative planning estimate. The exact pickup, stops and road conditions can change this.
Can I choose a vehicle other than a Sedan?
Yes. Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, Hycross, 12-seater Tempo Traveller, Urbania and bus options can be requested. The suitable category depends on passengers, luggage and availability.
What does the displayed starting price cover?
Where a starting price is shown, it is a vehicle-and-sightseeing transport estimate for four passengers sharing an AC Sedan. Hotels, tickets to the starting city and other trip-specific services are quoted separately.
Can the price change for my dates?
Yes. Demand, vehicle availability, travel dates, pickup and drop timings, route and requested services can change the quotation. Confirm the written inclusions and final amount before paying.
Can you arrange accommodation?
Yes, accommodation assistance can be requested. Select Yes in the form and share the room preference, adults, children and child ages so suitable stay options can be quoted.
Can you arrange my travel to Bangalore?
Yes. Request bus, train or flight assistance from your origin city. Available connections and fares are checked for the dates; a direct service to Bangalore is not assumed.
Are children included in the passenger count?
Yes. Enter all adults and children and provide each child’s age. Passenger count, luggage and any child-seat request help determine a suitable vehicle; confirm arrangements before booking.
What should I plan before visiting Varkala?
Two substantial transfer days leave limited destination time; consider rail or air arrival nearer the destination when the traveller wants a relaxed beach holiday. Travel windows allow planning only; traffic, breaks, weather and the exact pickup location change the actual journey.
When is my booking confirmed?
An enquiry or WhatsApp message is not a confirmed reservation. Booking is confirmed after the itinerary, available vehicle, inclusions, payment arrangements and cancellation conditions have been agreed.
